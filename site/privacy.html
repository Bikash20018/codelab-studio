CodeLab Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026 · applies to CodeLab Studio 2.0.0 and later

CodeLab Studio does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry of any kind. The application contains no networking code: it never sends anything anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Everything CodeLab Studio saves stays on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Preferences — your theme, font size, run mode and time limit.
  • Recently opened files — the paths of the last ten files you opened, so the File menu can list them.
  • Practice progress — which built-in exercises you have completed.
  • Crash-recovery snapshots — a periodic copy of the text in your open tabs and their file paths, so unsaved work survives a crash or a power cut.
  • Temporary build files — the source and executable of whatever you are compiling.

Preferences, recent files, practice progress and recovery snapshots live in %APPDATA%\CodeLabStudio. If you installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows redirects that to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<package>\LocalCache\Roaming\CodeLabStudio. Temporary build files live in %TEMP%\CodeLabStudio. You can delete any of these folders at any time; the app recreates what it needs.

Your code

Programs you write belong to you. They are compiled and run on your own computer, by the copy of GCC and GDB included in the app, as ordinary local programs. Your code is never uploaded, analysed remotely, or shared. No part of the application downloads or executes code from the internet.

When the app opens your browser

Two menu items open your default web browser: Help > About can open the developer's website, and Help > Licences and Source Code opens the licence notices stored on your own computer. Both happen only when you click them. Once your browser is open, that website's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Children

CodeLab Studio is built for students, including those under 13. Because it collects no information at all, there is nothing to gather, store or disclose about any user regardless of age, and no parental consent mechanism is needed.

Third-party components

The app bundles the MinGW-w64 build of GCC and GDB and LLVM's clang-format. These run locally as part of building and debugging your programs. None of them is configured to access the network, and the app never asks them to. Their licences and the corresponding source are listed at SOURCE.md.
